Refund Policy

Section 1: Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase, provided the product is unused and in original packaging.

Section 2: Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within 3-5 business days. The refund is issued to the original payment method.

Section 3: Late Refund Requests
Refund requests made after 30 days require manager approval and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

Section 4: Payment Verification
If a refund is delayed, it is often due to payment verification, which can take up to 5 additional business days for bank-related issues.
